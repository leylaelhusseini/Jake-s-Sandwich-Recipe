Ingredients

  • Cream cheese
  • Dill pickles from Prismo
  • Dill
  • Diced boiled egg
  • Bird from the window
  • Common cucumber
  • Sliced Roma tomato
  • Organic yellow onion
  • Tears for salt
  • Meat from the sous-vide (rosemary & thyme)
  • Bacon
  • Lobster soul

Instructions

  1. Toast the inside of the baguette with a torch.
  2. Spread a layer of cream cheese over the bottom bread.
  3. Lay down Prismo's dill pickles and sprinkle fresh dill on top.
  4. Add the diced boiled eggs and cooked bird meat.
  5. Layer the sliced cucumber, Roma tomato, and organic yellow onion.
  6. Add a pinch of salt using fresh tears.
  7. Stack the herb sous-vide meat and crispy bacon.
  8. Top it off with the soul of a lobster.
  9. Seal the sandwich with the top bun and enjoy.
